Features

Everything your social media workflow needs

Eight connected building blocks, set up around how your business actually works.

AI content generation

Generate post ideas, hooks, scripts and captions from your brand profile and monthly goals.

See an example

Input: “Back-to-school uniform sale, 15% off this week.” Output: five post ideas, three hooks and a short video script, ready for your review.

Platform-specific captions

Short and punchy for X, professional for LinkedIn, visual-first for Instagram, hook-led for TikTok.

See an example

One announcement becomes a short X post, a professional LinkedIn update and an Instagram caption with hashtags.

Content strategy development

Content pillars, audience notes and a posting rhythm matched to your goals and capacity.

See an example

A restaurant might get pillars like Menu spotlights, Behind the kitchen and Offers & events, plus a four-posts-a-week rhythm.

Content calendar

A shared calendar showing what goes out, where and when, linked to every draft and asset.

See an example

A shared Google Sheet or dashboard where each row is a post: date, platform, pillar, caption, asset link and status.

Creative briefs

Clear briefs for designers, photographers or your team: format, visuals, text on image and size.

See an example

“Carousel, 5 slides, warm colours, show the 3 new bowls. Text: New lunch menu from 12pm. Size 1080×1350.”

Approval workflows

Draft, review, approve or request changes, with notifications and a record of every decision.

See an example

Your manager gets a WhatsApp or email notification, taps Approve or adds a comment, and the status updates automatically.

Publishing assistance

Approved posts are queued in tools like Meta Business Suite or Buffer, or sent to you ready to post.

See an example

Approved posts are queued in Meta Business Suite or Buffer at the agreed times, or sent to you as a ready-to-post pack.

Performance reporting

Monthly summaries of reach, engagement and clicks, with practical notes for the next cycle.

See an example

At month end: a one-page summary of posts published, reach, engagement and clicks, with three suggestions for next month.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Does SocialFlow AI guarantee more followers or viral posts?

    No. SocialFlow AI helps you plan, create and organise content consistently and track results. Growth depends on your audience, offer, content quality and consistency, so we don't promise specific follower numbers or viral reach.

    Will anything be posted without my approval?

    No. Every post goes through an approval step. You or your team approve content before it is scheduled or published.

    Do I need technical skills to use it?

    No. Most of the workflow runs in tools you may already use, such as Google Sheets, Gmail or WhatsApp, and your setup includes a handover walkthrough.

    Which platforms are supported?

    Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and X. The number of platforms included depends on your package.

    Can content be written in Kiswahili?

    Yes. Drafts can be prepared in English, Kiswahili or a mix, and every draft is reviewed by a person before it goes out.

    What is the difference between the implementation fee and monthly support?

    The implementation fee is a one-off payment to set up your workflows. Monthly support is optional and covers updates, adjustments and help. Third-party tool subscriptions are paid directly to those providers.

    Who owns my content and accounts?

    You do. Your social media accounts, content and data stay in your name and your tools. Access you share for setup can be removed at any time.

    How long does setup take?

    It depends on the package and how many platforms and approvers are involved. You'll get a clear timeline after the consultation, before any work starts.
